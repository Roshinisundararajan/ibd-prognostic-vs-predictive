The gap between arms is the trial's average drug effect (odds ratio 2.09) and is the same proportion at every risk level. The model holds no term that varies the drug effect between patients, because none was supported by the data.

Research use only. Derived from one induction trial and not externally validated. In this cohort no baseline marker — clinical or transcriptomic — identified patients who benefit more from ustekinumab than from placebo, so these figures must not be used to choose between biologics.
Model specification and validation

Logistic regression fitted on all 550 patients, with uniform shrinkage (0.917) from 500 bootstrap resamples and the intercept re-estimated afterwards. Predictors were fixed before fitting, to the number 63 events support under Riley's criteria (4–6 parameters).

logit(p) = 2.677 + 0.738·ustekinumab − 0.381·Mayo − 0.581·antiTNF − 0.177·log2(calprotectin + 1)
